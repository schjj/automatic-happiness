🏝️ Colonies

A resource-building board game for 2–4 players

Quick Rules

  • 🎲 Roll dice → adjacent settlements collect resources
  • 🏠 Settlement = 1 VP, City = 2 VP
  • 🛣️ Longest road (≥5) = 2 VP
  • 🏆 First to 10 Victory Points wins
  • Build with: Wood+Brick=Road, Wood+Brick+Grain+Sheep=Settlement, Ore×3+Grain×2=City
